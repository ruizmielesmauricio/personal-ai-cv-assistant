# Moderation Error Risk Prediction System

## Project Summary

Developed a machine learning system to proactively identify moderation labels at high risk of generating future quality errors across multiple international markets.

The solution predicts future Leakage and Overkill risks one week in advance and automatically recommends the highest-risk labels requiring trainer intervention.

The project was designed to shift quality operations from a reactive approach to a proactive strategy by identifying emerging risks before they impact operational KPIs.

## Business Problem

Quality teams traditionally react to moderation errors after they occur.

This approach creates several challenges:

* Delayed trainer interventions.
* Repeated quality issues.
* Manual trend analysis.
* Difficulty prioritising training efforts.
* Increased impact on quality KPIs.

The objective was to create a predictive system capable of identifying high-risk labels before quality deterioration occurs.

## Operational Context

The solution was designed for moderation quality operations covering multiple European markets.

Markets included:

* Spain
* Italy
* France
* Germany

The system analyses historical moderation outcomes and predicts which labels are most likely to generate elevated error rates in the following week.

## Data Sources

Historical moderation quality data containing:

* Market
* Review date
* Error label
* Outcome
* Average Handling Time (AHT)

Outcomes included:

* Accurate
* Leakage
* Overkill

## Data Engineering

### Data Cleaning

* Removed extreme AHT outliers.
* Standardised label names.
* Converted review dates into weekly periods.
* Removed invalid records.
* Filtered operational noise.

### Weekly Aggregation

Created weekly label-level datasets including:

* Total reviews
* Leakage rate
* Overkill rate
* Average handling time
* Historical trends

### Feature Engineering

Generated operational indicators including:

#### Error Rates

* Leakage Rate
* Overkill Rate

#### Trend Features

* Previous week performance
* Rate deltas
* Rolling trends

#### Operational Features

* Total review volume
* Average handling time

#### Future Targets

Created future-week targets by shifting error rates forward in time.

This allowed the model to learn patterns that precede future quality deterioration.

## Machine Learning Models

Two independent XGBoost classification models were developed.

### Leakage Risk Model

Purpose:

Predict whether a label will become high-risk for Leakage in the following week.

### Overkill Risk Model

Purpose:

Predict whether a label will become high-risk for Overkill in the following week.

## Model Evaluation

### Leakage Classifier

Performance:

* Accuracy: 92%
* Recall: 97% for high-risk labels

Business Interpretation:

The model successfully identified most future leakage risks, reducing the probability of missing critical training opportunities.

### Overkill Classifier

Performance:

* Accuracy: 98%
* Recall improved through threshold tuning.

Business Interpretation:

The model identified emerging overkill risks while maintaining low false-positive rates.

## Risk Scoring Framework

Model probabilities alone were not sufficient for operational prioritisation.

A custom risk score was developed combining:

### Risk Probability

Probability generated by the machine learning model.

### Operational Volume

Labels with higher review volumes receive greater weighting.

### Trend Severity

Labels with worsening performance trends receive additional weighting.

Final Formula:

Risk Score =
Probability × Volume Score × Trend Severity

This approach prioritises labels that are:

* Likely to become problematic.
* Affecting a large number of reviews.
* Showing worsening behaviour.

## Recommendation Engine

After scoring all labels:

* Labels are ranked by risk score.
* Top two labels are selected for each market.
* Recommendations are generated automatically.

Outputs include:

* Market
* Error Type
* Label
* Risk Probability
* Risk Score
* Trainer Recommendation

## Key Learning

Model probability should not be interpreted as a deterministic forecast.

The probability represents a risk signal that helps prioritise attention and intervention efforts.

Combining machine learning outputs with operational context produced significantly more useful recommendations than relying on model predictions alone.
