## Company

## Location

Dublin, Ireland

## Period

## Overview

Supported operational excellence initiatives through quality analysis, process improvement, reporting, and workflow automation.

## Key Responsibilities

* Conducted quality audits and performance analysis.
* Performed root cause analysis to identify operational inefficiencies.
* Gathered stakeholder feedback and documented business requirements.
* Built Power BI dashboards using Dataverse.
* Evaluated process performance using Lean Six Sigma principles.
* Produced performance reports and operational insights.

* Improved workflow visibility.

Built Power BI dashboards integrating Dataverse data to support performance monitoring and operational management.

Business Impact:

* Real-time KPI visibility.
* Faster identification of performance issues.
* Improved reporting consistency.

### Process Optimisation

Conducted data-driven root cause analysis and Lean Six Sigma investigations.

Business Impact:

* Reduced operational inefficiencies.
* Improved service quality.
* Saved approximately 5 hours per week through process improvements.
