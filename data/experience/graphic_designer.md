# Graphic Designer


## Period

2007 - 2013

## Overview

Responsible for creating corporate images, banners, animations and webdesign as a coordinator and project manager of an advertisement company in Venezuela. 

## Key Responsibilities

* Coordinate with the team to provide graphic solutions to our clients.
* Create visually impacting images to increase clients' revenue. 
* Liase with clients to provide posible solutions to their needs. 
* Brief the team to provide all the necessary information provided by the client.
* Design useful yet impacting websites. 



## Technologies & Tools
* Adobe Suite
* HTML
* CSS
* 3D Blender

## Skills demonstrated
* Deep understanding of User Experience
* Implementation of useful User Interface
* Visually engaging graphics
