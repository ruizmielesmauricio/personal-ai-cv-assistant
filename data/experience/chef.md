# Sous Chef

## Company

Pitt Bros and 105 Cafe

## Location

Dublin, Ireland

## Period

March 2024 – Present

## Overview

responsible for supporting kitchen operations, food preparation, team supervision, inventory management, and quality control in a high-volume hospitality environment. Worked closely with the Head Chef to maintain food standards, ensure efficient service delivery, and uphold food safety and hygiene regulations.

## Key Responsibilities

* Supervise daily kitchen operations and coordinate food preparation activities.
* Support menu planning, recipe development, and seasonal menu updates.
* Monitor food quality, presentation, and portion control to ensure consistency.
* Train, mentor, and oversee junior chefs and kitchen staff.
* Manage stock levels, inventory rotation, and supplier deliveries.
* Ensure compliance with food safety, HACCP, and health & safety regulations.
* Coordinate kitchen workflow during peak service periods to maintain efficiency.
* Minimize food waste through effective inventory and production planning.
* Assist in cost control initiatives, including food cost monitoring and labor optimization.
* Collaborate with front-of-house teams to ensure excellent customer experiences.
